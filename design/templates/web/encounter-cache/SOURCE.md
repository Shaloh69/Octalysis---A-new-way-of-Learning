**Why the Clock Bench's reference and not a new one.** The Cache Tuner is the
same kind of surface: one framed panel of labelled controls that take effect
at once, over the live world, with readouts beside them. A second reference
would have invited a second look for the same kind of bench. What differs is
the content (a mapping choice as radio buttons, and a 24-bit address drawn as
three fields), which is ours; `SPEC.md` says what.
