Claim your OCTA account

CPE 412 · Computer Architecture and Organization · University of Cebu

Your instructor has already added you to the class roster. Claiming your account takes about two minutes, and you only do it once. After that you sign in with your student ID.

Scan to open
octa-web-dusky.vercel.app

Before you start, have these ready

  1. Your student ID, exactly as it is on your registration form.
  2. An email address you use. You can sign in with it too.
  3. A password of at least 10 characters. A long phrase is stronger, and easier to remember, than a short password full of symbols.

1Open the site

Go to octa-web-dusky.vercel.app on your phone or laptop, and tap Claim your account.

2Fill in the form

Your student ID, your email, then your password twice. Then tap Claim account.

3Account claimed

You will see Account claimed. Tap Go to sign in. No confirmation email is needed.

4Sign in

Enter your student ID (or your email) and the password you just set, then tap Sign in.

5You are in

Your star system opens: one planet for each chapter of the course. From now on, use Sign in every time.

6Keep it safe

Your account is yours alone: your answers and checks are graded. Never share your password, and never claim a classmate's ID.

Bookmark the site or add it to your home screen: octa-web-dusky.vercel.app

Sign out on a shared or borrowed computer, from the top right of the map.

If something goes wrong

"That student ID could not be used"

That student ID could not be used. Check the ID on your registration form, or ask your instructor if it has already been claimed.

  • Check every digit of your student ID against your registration form.
  • If the ID is right, it may already have been claimed, or it may not be on the roster yet. Tell your instructor; do not keep trying.
  • The message is the same for every one of these on purpose, so nobody can use the site to find out which IDs exist.
  • After several tries in a minute the site makes you wait a minute. That is normal.

Claim account will not press

  • A box is still empty, or your password is shorter than 10 characters (the note under it changes to a warning), or the two passwords do not match yet ("These do not match yet." appears under the second one).

"Check your ID and password" when you sign in

  • Check your student ID, and that Caps Lock is off. You can also sign in with the email you used.
  • Have you claimed your account yet? Sign in only works after step 3.

You forgot your password

  • There is no "reset by email" for students yet. Tell your instructor, who can help you get back in.

Which device?

  • Any up-to-date phone or laptop browser works: Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
