  lights.png   parallax-forest-lights        16.1% -- dithered god-rays
  front.png    parallax-forest-front-trees   39.2% coverage

`lights` is drawn BETWEEN mid and front: shafts fall in front of the middle
distance and behind the nearest trunks, which is what makes them read as light
in the air rather than as a glow pasted on top.

Nothing is resized or recoloured.
