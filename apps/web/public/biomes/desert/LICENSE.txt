A CC0 derivative of a non-CC0 original is a problem the derivative's own licence
field cannot fix, so the chain is walked rather than assumed. The same check
cleared the previous cave pack's PWL original.

NO LAYER IS FULLY OPAQUE, which is unusual and is why this biome genuinely needs
the sky gradient from `--biome-sky-*` (BIOME-AND-LOADING-SPEC.md 2e, slot 1).

The pack also ships `water` variants with a river through the mid ground. Not
vendored: this biome sits behind reading content, and a bright water band across
the middle of the frame competes with the panels for the attention they need.

Nothing is resized or recoloured.
