WHY OPENGAMEART AND NOT ITCH.IO

An earlier attempt at this biome used ansimuz's *Warped Caves* from itch.io and
rejected it: that page carries no licence statement from the author, and the
CC-BY-3.0 people cite for it is in a seven-year-old USER COMMENT written by
somebody else. A claim in a comment thread is not a licence.

This is the same artist, and the licence problem is gone, because OpenGameArt
records the licence as structured metadata ON THE WORK. It can be read, cited
and re-verified by anyone later. That is the whole reason this project prefers
OGA as a source.

The pack also ships loose plant props (palm, plant-big, plant). They were
vendored and then removed on 7 Sep 2026: they are platformer floor decoration,
and at page scale behind reading content they read as a hedge. Not vendored.

Nothing is resized or recoloured. The layers are 240px tall and are drawn
scaled up, which is correct for pixel art and is why `smooth` is off for this
biome.
